Day 93: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher gives a multiplication; children show the answer with fingers.

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-09): Multiplies a two-digit number by a one-digit number and knows tables up to 10

Activity (OPS-09-A2): Table quiz circle, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk

  1. Children sit in a circle.
  2. Teacher points to a child and says: 7 times 6. Child answers 42. If correct, stay seated. If wrong, stand up. Standing children quietly practice their tables on fingers while waiting for another turn.
  3. Go around the circle covering tables 6, 7, 8, 9.
  4. After one round, standing children get a second chance.
  5. End by writing 5 table facts on the board that many children got wrong. Chant them together 3 times.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-16): Uses decimal notation for money and lengths (metres and centimetres)

Activity (OPS-16-A2): Length as decimals, 12 min. Materials: string, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Write on board: 1 metre = 100 centimetres. So 1 cm = 0.01 m.
  2. Measure a stick with string: 1 metre and 35 cm = 1.35 m.
  3. Measure the desk: about 75 cm = 0.75 m.
  4. Children measure 3 things using a 1-metre string and write in metres with decimals on their slates.
  5. Add two lengths: 1.35 m + 0.75 m = 2.10 m.

Check: Read this decimal and tell me its value.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A1): Unitary method introduction, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on board: 5 notebooks cost 60 rupees. What is the cost of 1 notebook? 60 ÷ 5 = 12 rupees. What is the cost of 8 notebooks? 12 x 8 = 96 rupees.
  2. This is the unitary method: first find the cost or value of ONE unit, then multiply to find the cost of many.
  3. Children solve: 3 kg of sugar costs 135 rupees. Cost of 7 kg? (135÷3=45, 45x7=315.)
  4. Do 3 more problems on slates.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
