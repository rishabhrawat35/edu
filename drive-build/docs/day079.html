Day 79: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher writes a subtraction; children solve on slates.

Rotation

Teacher begins with A (15 min); rotates to C (15 min); finishes with B (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-07): Identifies half and quarter of a whole object or collection

Activity (OPS-07-A1): Paper folding halves and quarters, 12 min. Materials: paper, chalk, blackboard

  1. Give each child a piece of paper.
  2. Fold it in half. Open it. How many equal parts? 2. Colour one part. That is one half.
  3. Fold again into 4 equal parts. Open it. How many parts? 4. Colour one part. That is one quarter.
  4. Write on board: 1/2 means 1 out of 2 equal parts. 1/4 means 1 out of 4 equal parts.
  5. Fold a different shaped paper and find halves again.

Check: Show me: Identifies half and quarter of a whole object or c

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-14): Solves multi-step everyday problems using the four operations

Activity (OPS-14-A1): Multi-step market problem, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A farmer sells 25 kg of tomatoes at 18 rupees per kg and 15 kg of onions at 22 rupees per kg. How much does he earn in total?
  2. Step 1: 25 x 18 = 450. Step 2: 15 x 22 = 330. Step 3: 450 + 330 = 780.
  3. Children solve 2 more multi-step problems on slates.
  4. Ask: what if he spent 500 on seeds? What is his profit? 780-500=280. That is a 4-step problem.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A1): Unitary method introduction, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on board: 5 notebooks cost 60 rupees. What is the cost of 1 notebook? 60 ÷ 5 = 12 rupees. What is the cost of 8 notebooks? 12 x 8 = 96 rupees.
  2. This is the unitary method: first find the cost or value of ONE unit, then multiply to find the cost of many.
  3. Children solve: 3 kg of sugar costs 135 rupees. Cost of 7 kg? (135÷3=45, 45x7=315.)
  4. Do 3 more problems on slates.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
