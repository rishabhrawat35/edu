Day 71: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Continue the number pattern: 2, 4, 6, ...

Rotation

Teacher begins with A (15 min); rotates to C (15 min); finishes with B (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-04): Reads and draws bar graphs and tables and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-04-A1): Bar graph from data, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write data on the board: favourite sport — cricket 12, football 8, kabaddi 6, kho-kho 4.
  2. Draw axes: sports on the bottom, numbers on the side (scale of 2).
  3. Draw bars together — one per sport.
  4. Ask: which bar is tallest? How many more chose cricket than kho-kho?
  5. Children draw the same bar graph on slates.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-05): Draws and reads bar graphs and tables and finds simple averages

Activity (DAT-05-A3): Temperature table, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a table: city names and temperatures for one week (made-up data for 5 cities, 5 days).
  2. Ask: find the average temperature for Delhi.
  3. Which city is hottest on average? Coolest?
  4. Draw a bar graph of average temperatures for each city.
  5. Children calculate and draw on slates.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-06): Finds and describes number patterns and extends them

Activity (DAT-06-A3): Create your own pattern, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Each child creates a number pattern with a hidden rule and writes 5 terms on their slate.
  2. Pass the slate to a partner.
  3. The partner writes the next 3 terms and the rule.
  4. Check together.
  5. Share the hardest patterns with the class on the board.
  6. Vote on the trickiest pattern.

Check: What comes next in this pattern?
