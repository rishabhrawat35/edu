Day 70: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher draws a pattern on the board; children say what comes next.

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-04): Reads and draws bar graphs and tables and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-04-A3): Collect and graph, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Give each child a task: count the number of windows, doors, and benches in the school.
  2. Children report numbers — write on the board.
  3. Together draw a bar graph of the data.
  4. Choose a scale that fits (each square = 1 or = 2).
  5. Ask: why did we choose that scale? What if each square was 5?

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-05): Draws and reads bar graphs and tables and finds simple averages

Activity (DAT-05-A2): Bar graph with average line, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a bar graph on the board: marks of 5 students in a test.
  2. Calculate the average mark.
  3. Draw a horizontal line at the average on the graph.
  4. Ask: how many students scored above average? Below average?
  5. Discuss: what does the average line tell us? Does anyone have exactly the average score?

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-06): Finds and describes number patterns and extends them

Activity (DAT-06-A2): Pattern in tables, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the multiplication table of 9 on the board: 9, 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90.
  2. Ask: what do you notice about the digits? (tens go up by 1, ones go down by 1, digits always add to 9).
  3. Check: does the same pattern work for the table of 3? (digits add to 3, 6, 9, 3, 6, 9...).
  4. Children look for patterns in the table of 5 and write them on slates.
  5. Discuss: why do patterns appear in multiplication tables?

Check: What comes next in this pattern?
