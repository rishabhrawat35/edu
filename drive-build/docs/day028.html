Day 28: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate how many cups of water fill a bucket.

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-02): Names days of the week, sorts events by time (before, after, today, tomorrow)

Activity (MEA-02-A1): Days of the week song, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the seven days on the board in order.
  2. Sing or chant them together three times.
  3. Erase one day — children say which is missing.
  4. Ask: what day is today? What was yesterday? What will tomorrow be?
  5. Children write the days on their slates in order.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A1): Clock reading drill, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a clock on the board showing 2:30.
  2. Ask: what time is it? How do you know?
  3. Change to 2:45 — ask again.
  4. Show 5 different times (mix of :00, :15, :30, :45) — children write each on slates.
  5. Ask: how many minutes from 2:30 to 3:00?

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-11): Finds the perimeter of simple shapes and the area of shapes by counting squares

Activity (MEA-11-A1): String perimeter, 15 min. Materials: string, ruler, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle on the board: length 4 units, width 3 units.
  2. Ask: how far would an ant walk around the edge?
  3. Count together: 4 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 14 units.
  4. Children draw 3 shapes on slates with sides marked and find the perimeter.
  5. Measure the perimeter of their slate with a string, then measure the string with a ruler.

Check: Count these objects - how many are there?
