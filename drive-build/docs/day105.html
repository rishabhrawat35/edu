Day 105: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate: how many hand-spans is the teacher's desk?

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A3): Time your tasks, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk

  1. Ask one child to write the alphabet on the slate.
  2. Another child counts to 60 (roughly 1 minute). How many letters did the first child write?
  3. Ask: if she writes that many letters per minute, how long for all 26?
  4. Repeat with another task: how many times can you clap in 30 seconds?
  5. Discuss: minutes are for longer tasks, seconds for short ones.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A3): Recipe scaling, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a recipe on the board: 500 g flour, 250 ml milk, 100 g sugar (makes 1 batch).
  2. Ask: how much of each for 2 batches? For half a batch?
  3. Children calculate on slates.
  4. Write answers in both g and kg, ml and l where possible.
  5. Discuss: when does a cook think in grams and when in kilograms?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A3): Design a garden, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Say: you have 24 metres of fencing (perimeter = 24 m).
  2. Design a rectangular garden. What could the length and width be?
  3. Children find all whole-number possibilities on slates: 1x11, 2x10, 3x9, 4x8, 5x7, 6x6.
  4. Calculate the area for each.
  5. Which rectangle gives the most area? (6x6 = 36). Discuss: a square gives the most area for a fixed perimeter.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
