Day 44: Shapes and Space

Whole class opening (5 min)

Look around the room - name every shape you can see.

Rotation

Teacher begins with A (15 min); rotates to C (15 min); finishes with B (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (GEO-04): Identifies angles, lines and the faces and edges of 3-D shapes; uses direction and grids

Activity (GEO-04-A3): Grid treasure map, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a 5x5 grid on the board and label columns A-E and rows 1-5.
  2. Place a chalk mark at B3 — this is the treasure.
  3. Give directions: start at A1, go right 1 step, up 2 steps — where are you?
  4. Children take turns giving directions to reach the treasure.
  5. On slates, children draw their own 4x4 grid and place a treasure for a partner to find.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (GEO-06): Identifies and measures angles and describes properties of 2-D and 3-D shapes

Activity (GEO-06-A3): Angle in the clock, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a clock face on the board showing 3:00 — the hands form a right angle.
  2. Ask: what angle at 6:00? (straight line, 180 degrees) At 12:00? (0 degrees)
  3. Show 1:00 — is the angle more or less than 90?
  4. Children draw 4 clock faces on slates showing different times and estimate the angle.
  5. Compare estimates as a class.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (GEO-07): Finds and uses symmetry, tiling and reflection in designs

Activity (GEO-07-A1): Reflection drawing, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a dotted vertical line on the board.
  2. Draw a simple shape on the left side.
  3. Ask a child to draw its mirror image on the right.
  4. Check: each point should be the same distance from the line.
  5. Children draw 3 reflection pairs on slates.
  6. Discuss: reflection flips left-right but not up-down.

Check: Draw the line of symmetry on this shape.
