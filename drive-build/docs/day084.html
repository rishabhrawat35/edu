Day 84: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher asks: what is heavier, your slate or your bag?

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-05): Reads time to the hour and half hour and knows days, weeks and months

Activity (MEA-05-A3): Month and week count, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the 12 months on the board.
  2. Chant them together.
  3. Ask: how many months in a year? How many days in a week?
  4. Ask: which month is your birthday? Count how many months until your birthday.
  5. Children write today's full date on slates: day, date, month, year.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A3): Duration word problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A film starts at 4:30 PM and lasts 2 hours 15 minutes. When does it end?
  2. Solve together on the board step by step.
  3. Write 3 more problems mixing hours and minutes.
  4. Children solve on slates.
  5. Check: count forward from start time by hours first, then minutes.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A3): Design a garden, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Say: you have 24 metres of fencing (perimeter = 24 m).
  2. Design a rectangular garden. What could the length and width be?
  3. Children find all whole-number possibilities on slates: 1x11, 2x10, 3x9, 4x8, 5x7, 6x6.
  4. Calculate the area for each.
  5. Which rectangle gives the most area? (6x6 = 36). Discuss: a square gives the most area for a fixed perimeter.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
