Day 38: Numbers and Place Value

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher shows fingers; children say the number they see.

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (NUM-03): Compares and orders numbers up to 99 (more, less, equal, smallest to largest)

Activity (NUM-03-A3): Equal or not equal, 12 min. Materials: bottle caps, slates, chalk

  1. Give each pair of children 2 piles of bottle caps (different amounts up to 50 each).
  2. Each child counts their pile.
  3. They compare: who has more? How many more? Are they equal?
  4. Children write on their slates: [number] is more than [number] OR [number] = [number].
  5. Swap piles and repeat.

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?

Group B, Middle

Skill (NUM-06): Reads, writes and compares numbers up to 9999 using place value

Activity (NUM-06-A3): Before and after four-digit numbers, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a four-digit number on the board (e.g. 5000).
  2. Ask: what comes just before? Just after?
  3. Children write answers on slates.
  4. Now write 2999. Ask: what comes next? Discuss the jump to 3000.
  5. Do 6 rounds focusing on numbers near hundreds and thousands boundaries (e.g. 4099 to 4100 and 6999 to 7000).

Check: Read this number and write the next one.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (NUM-09): Finds factors, multiples and uses simple divisibility tests

Activity (NUM-09-A3): Divisibility tests, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: a number is divisible by 2 if it ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8.
  2. Write 10 numbers. Children mark on slates which are divisible by 2.
  3. Now teach: divisible by 5 if it ends in 0 or 5. Children test the same 10 numbers.
  4. Teach: divisible by 3 if the sum of digits is divisible by 3. Show with 123: 1+2+3=6, and 6 is divisible by 3, so yes. Spend extra time on this rule — do 3 examples together on the board before children try alone.
  5. Children test 5 numbers for divisibility by 3.

Check: What is this number times that number?
