Day 27: Shapes and Space

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher draws a shape; children name it and count its sides.

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (GEO-03): Describes 2-D and 3-D shapes in detail and finds lines of symmetry in simple figures

Activity (GEO-03-A2): Shape description game, 12 min. Materials: paper, chalk, blackboard

  1. One child picks a shape card (drawn on paper) and hides it.
  2. They describe it: number of sides, corners, whether sides are equal, whether it has symmetry.
  3. Other children draw the shape they think it is on their slates before the answer is revealed.
  4. The describer shows the card. Compare with what children drew.
  5. Repeat with 3-D shapes: describe faces, edges, corners.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (GEO-05): Finds symmetry in shapes and extends patterns involving shapes and numbers

Activity (GEO-05-A2): Symmetry in letters, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the alphabet on the board in capital letters.
  2. Ask: which letters have a vertical line of symmetry? (A, M, T, U, V, W, Y)
  3. Which have a horizontal line? (B, C, D, E, H, K)
  4. Which have both? (H, I, O, X)
  5. Children write 5 letters on slates and draw the lines of symmetry.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (GEO-07): Finds and uses symmetry, tiling and reflection in designs

Activity (GEO-07-A2): Rangoli tiling, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Show a simple rangoli pattern on the board using one repeated shape.
  2. Identify the base shape and the symmetry (rotation, reflection).
  3. Children create their own rangoli on slates using triangles or squares as the base tile.
  4. Exchange with a partner and identify: what is the base shape? What symmetry does it have?
  5. Best designs drawn on the board.

Check: Draw the line of symmetry on this shape.
