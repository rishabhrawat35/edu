Day 113: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate: how many hand-spans is the teacher's desk?

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-08): Works with money including bills, change and simple transactions

Activity (MEA-08-A2): Bill checker, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a pretend bill on the board: 3 items with prices.
  2. Add them up together.
  3. Write the total. Now check: is the total correct?
  4. Give children 3 bills on slates (some with wrong totals).
  5. They check each and mark correct or incorrect.

Check: How much money is this?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-13): Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units

Activity (MEA-13-A2): Capacity estimation, 15 min. Materials: containers, cups, water, blackboard, chalk

  1. Show 3 containers of different shapes but similar size.
  2. Ask: which holds the most water? Estimate.
  3. Pour water from one to another to compare.
  4. Measure each with a cup and record in ml on the board.
  5. Discuss: a tall thin bottle and a short wide one can hold the same amount.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A3): Design a garden, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Say: you have 24 metres of fencing (perimeter = 24 m).
  2. Design a rectangular garden. What could the length and width be?
  3. Children find all whole-number possibilities on slates: 1x11, 2x10, 3x9, 4x8, 5x7, 6x6.
  4. Calculate the area for each.
  5. Which rectangle gives the most area? (6x6 = 36). Discuss: a square gives the most area for a fixed perimeter.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
