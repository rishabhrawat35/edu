Day 30: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Show me a coin - what is it worth?

Rotation

Teacher begins with A (15 min); rotates to C (15 min); finishes with B (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-02): Names days of the week, sorts events by time (before, after, today, tomorrow)

Activity (MEA-02-A3): Yesterday-today-tomorrow, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write today's day on the board.
  2. Ask: what day was yesterday? Write it. What day is tomorrow? Write it.
  3. Ask 5 children: what did you do yesterday? What will you do tomorrow?
  4. On slates, children write: Yesterday was ___. Today is ___. Tomorrow will be ___.
  5. Discuss: does tomorrow always come after today?

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A3): Time your tasks, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk

  1. Ask one child to write the alphabet on the slate.
  2. Another child counts to 60 (roughly 1 minute). How many letters did the first child write?
  3. Ask: if she writes that many letters per minute, how long for all 26?
  4. Repeat with another task: how many times can you clap in 30 seconds?
  5. Discuss: minutes are for longer tasks, seconds for short ones.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-11): Finds the perimeter of simple shapes and the area of shapes by counting squares

Activity (MEA-11-A3): Perimeter walk, 15 min. Materials: chalk, measuring tape, old newspapers

  1. Use chalk to draw a rectangle on the classroom floor (2m x 3m).
  2. Children walk along the edges and count their steps.
  3. Measure each side with a measuring tape.
  4. Add the four sides for perimeter.
  5. Cover the inside with old newspapers — count how many sheets fit for a rough area.

Check: Count these objects - how many are there?
