Day 22: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Story problem: "I had 5 mangoes and ate 2, how many left?"

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-02): Solves simple everyday addition and subtraction word problems with small numbers

Activity (OPS-02-A3): Classroom counting problems, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Count how many boys are sitting in the room. Write the number on the board.
  2. Count how many girls. Write it.
  3. Ask: how many children in total? Children add on their slates.
  4. Ask: how many more boys than girls (or girls than boys)? Children subtract.
  5. Make 2 more problems using real things: chairs and children, windows and doors.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-09): Multiplies a two-digit number by a one-digit number and knows tables up to 10

Activity (OPS-09-A3): Multiply to find total, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw 4 bags on the board. Write 16 on each bag (16 oranges per bag).
  2. Ask: how many oranges in total? 16 x 4. Children solve on slate.
  3. Draw 3 rows of 25 chairs. How many chairs? 25 x 3.
  4. 5 packets with 18 biscuits each. How many biscuits?
  5. Children make up their own story and solve. Share one with the class.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-16): Uses decimal notation for money and lengths (metres and centimetres)

Activity (OPS-16-A3): Place value of decimals, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a place value chart on board: Tens | Ones | . | Tenths | Hundredths.
  2. Write 23.47: 2 tens, 3 ones, 4 tenths, 7 hundredths.
  3. Write 5.09: 5 ones, 0 tenths, 9 hundredths.
  4. Children read 4 numbers and place them in the chart on their slates: 10.50, 3.08, 45.16, 0.72.
  5. Compare: which is bigger, 3.5 or 3.08? 3.5 because 5 tenths > 0 tenths.

Check: Read this decimal and tell me its value.
