Day 66: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

What day is today? What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-04): Measures length, weight and capacity using simple standard units (cm, m, kg, litre)

Activity (MEA-04-A2): Water pouring, 15 min. Materials: bottles, cups, water, blackboard, chalk

  1. Show a 1-litre bottle (or any large bottle marked at the top) and a small cup.
  2. Pour cups of water into the bottle and count how many fill it.
  3. Write on the board: 1 litre = ___ cups.
  4. Now pour half a litre — how many cups?
  5. Children estimate: how many cups to fill their water bottle? Then test it.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-09): Measures and converts units of length (cm, m, km), weight (g, kg) and capacity (ml, l)

Activity (MEA-09-A2): Measure the classroom, 15 min. Materials: string (at least 5 metres), metre ruler, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Children measure the length and width of the room in metres using a long string and a metre ruler (mark the string at 1-metre intervals, then count marks).
  2. Measure the desk height in cm.
  3. Convert: the room is ___ m = ___ cm.
  4. Write all measurements on the board.
  5. Ask: would you measure the distance to the nearest town in cm, m, or km? Why?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-13): Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units

Activity (MEA-13-A2): Capacity estimation, 15 min. Materials: containers, cups, water, blackboard, chalk

  1. Show 3 containers of different shapes but similar size.
  2. Ask: which holds the most water? Estimate.
  3. Pour water from one to another to compare.
  4. Measure each with a cup and record in ml on the board.
  5. Discuss: a tall thin bottle and a short wide one can hold the same amount.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?
