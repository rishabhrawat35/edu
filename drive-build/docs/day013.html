Day 13: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Quick quiz: how many days in a week? How many months in a year?

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-01): Compares objects by length, weight or capacity using everyday words and non-standard units

Activity (MEA-01-A3): Handspan measuring, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk, sticks

  1. Show how to use a handspan to measure the desk.
  2. Children measure the desk width in handspans and write the number on their slates.
  3. Measure the same desk with a stick — how many sticks?
  4. Ask: why are the handspan numbers different for different children?
  5. Discuss: we need a unit everyone agrees on.

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-06): Measures and estimates length, weight and capacity; converts between larger and smaller units

Activity (MEA-06-A3): Litre converter, 12 min. Materials: bottles, cups, water, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: 1 litre = 1000 ml.
  2. Show a 1-litre bottle and a small 250 ml cup.
  3. Ask: how many cups in a litre? (4).
  4. Write 3 problems: 2 litres = ___ ml; 3000 ml = ___ litres; half litre = ___ ml.
  5. Children solve on slates.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A3): Duration word problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A film starts at 4:30 PM and lasts 2 hours 15 minutes. When does it end?
  2. Solve together on the board step by step.
  3. Write 3 more problems mixing hours and minutes.
  4. Children solve on slates.
  5. Check: count forward from start time by hours first, then minutes.

Check: What time does this clock show?
