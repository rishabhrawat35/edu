Day 41: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Finger addition: teacher shows fingers on both hands, class adds.

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-03): Adds and subtracts two-digit numbers with and without carrying or borrowing

Activity (OPS-03-A2): Borrowing with sticks, 15 min. Materials: sticks, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Give bundles of 10 sticks and loose sticks to pairs of children.
  2. Write 52-27 on the board. Children lay out 5 bundles and 2 sticks.
  3. Ask: can you take 7 sticks from 2? No. Open one bundle. Now you have 4 bundles and 12 sticks.
  4. Take away 7 sticks: 12-7=5. Take away 2 bundles: 4-2=2. Answer: 25.
  5. Do 3 more problems with materials then on slate without materials.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-10): Divides a number by a one-digit number and links division to multiplication

Activity (OPS-10-A2): Division with remainder, 12 min. Materials: stones, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Give each group 17 stones. Share equally among 3 children. Each gets 5, and 2 are left over.
  2. Write: 17 divided by 3 = 5 remainder 2.
  3. Try 23 stones among 4: each gets 5, remainder 3.
  4. Try 30 stones among 6: each gets 5, no remainder.
  5. Children solve on slate: 29 divided by 4, 37 divided by 5, 50 divided by 8.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-17): Does the four operations on large numbers and estimates answers

Activity (OPS-17-A2): Estimation challenge, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 5 problems on the board (large-number addition and subtraction).
  2. Children do NOT solve exactly. They round each number to the nearest thousand and add or subtract. Write only the estimate.
  3. Then solve one exactly and compare. How close was the estimate?
  4. Discuss: when is estimation useful? (Checking your work, quick shopping math, planning.)

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
