Day 82: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

What day is today? What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-05): Reads time to the hour and half hour and knows days, weeks and months

Activity (MEA-05-A1): Clock face drawing, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a clock face on the board with numbers 1-12.
  2. Show the hour hand pointing to 3 — ask: what time? (3 o'clock).
  3. Show half past 3 — the minute hand points to 6.
  4. Children draw a clock on their slates and show 5 o'clock.
  5. Call out 4 times — children draw hands on their clocks for each.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A1): 24-hour clock, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a number line from 0 to 24 on the board.
  2. Mark 12 — this is noon.
  3. Write: 1 PM = 13:00, 2 PM = 14:00. Ask children to continue.
  4. Give 5 times in 12-hour format — children write in 24-hour on slates.
  5. Give 5 times in 24-hour — children write in 12-hour.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A1): Rectangle perimeter formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle: length 6 cm, width 4 cm on the board.
  2. Perimeter = 6+4+6+4 = 20 cm. Shorter way: 2 x (6+4) = 20.
  3. Write the formula: P = 2 x (l + w).
  4. Give 4 rectangles with different lengths and widths.
  5. Children use the formula on slates. Check by counting.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
