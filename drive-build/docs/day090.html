Day 90: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Story problem: "I had 5 mangoes and ate 2, how many left?"

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-08): Adds and subtracts three-digit numbers including with carrying and borrowing

Activity (OPS-08-A2): Three-digit subtraction with borrowing, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 503-247 on board in columns.
  2. Ones: 3-7 cannot do. Tens: 0, cannot borrow. Go to hundreds: 5 becomes 4, tens becomes 10, then tens becomes 9 and ones becomes 13.
  3. Now 13-7=6, 9-4=5, 4-2=2. Answer: 256.
  4. Walk through slowly. Children solve 3 problems on slate: 600-253, 412-178, 735-368.
  5. Pairs check each other's work.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-15): Understands equivalent fractions and adds and subtracts fractions with the same denominator

Activity (OPS-15-A2): Adding fractions with same denominator, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on board: 1/5 + 2/5 = ?. Draw a strip divided into 5 parts. Colour 1 part then 2 more. Total coloured: 3 parts out of 5. So 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5.
  2. Rule: when the bottom number is the same, just add the top numbers.
  3. Children solve on slates: 2/7 + 3/7, 1/4 + 2/4, 3/8 + 4/8.
  4. Now try subtraction: 5/6 - 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2. Simplify if possible.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A1): Unitary method introduction, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on board: 5 notebooks cost 60 rupees. What is the cost of 1 notebook? 60 ÷ 5 = 12 rupees. What is the cost of 8 notebooks? 12 x 8 = 96 rupees.
  2. This is the unitary method: first find the cost or value of ONE unit, then multiply to find the cost of many.
  3. Children solve: 3 kg of sugar costs 135 rupees. Cost of 7 kg? (135÷3=45, 45x7=315.)
  4. Do 3 more problems on slates.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
