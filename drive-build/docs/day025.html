Day 25: Shapes and Space

Whole class opening (5 min)

Point to something round, something square, something triangular.

Rotation

Teacher begins with A (15 min); rotates to C (15 min); finishes with B (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (GEO-02): Sorts shapes by properties and identifies faces, corners, straight and curved lines

Activity (GEO-02-A1): Shape sorting race, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk

  1. Draw 10 shapes on the board: mix of circles, triangles, rectangles, squares, curved shapes.
  2. Draw two columns: straight sides and curved sides.
  3. Call children one by one to point at a shape and say which column it goes in.
  4. After sorting, ask: what do all the straight-sided shapes have in common?
  5. Count faces and corners of each group together.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (GEO-04): Identifies angles, lines and the faces and edges of 3-D shapes; uses direction and grids

Activity (GEO-04-A1): Angle arms, 12 min. Materials: book, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Two children stand and stretch their arms to form an angle.
  2. Ask the class: is this angle big or small?
  3. Demonstrate a right angle using the corner of a book.
  4. Compare arm-angles to the book corner: bigger, smaller or same?
  5. Draw right angle, acute and obtuse on the board and label them.
  6. Children draw each type on their slates.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (GEO-06): Identifies and measures angles and describes properties of 2-D and 3-D shapes

Activity (GEO-06-A1): Paper angle measurer, 12 min. Materials: paper, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Fold a square piece of paper to make a right angle (90 degrees).
  2. Fold again to make 45 degrees.
  3. Use the paper angle to measure corners of objects in the room.
  4. Record on slates: object name, angle type (right, less than right, more than right).
  5. Draw the angles on the board and label approximate degrees.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
