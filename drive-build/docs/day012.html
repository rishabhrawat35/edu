Day 12: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Show me a coin - what is it worth?

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-01): Compares objects by length, weight or capacity using everyday words and non-standard units

Activity (MEA-01-A2): Heavy or light, 10 min. Materials: stones, leaves, bottles, water

  1. Hold a stone in one hand and a leaf in the other.
  2. Ask: which is heavier? Which is lighter?
  3. Pass both around — each child says heavy or light.
  4. Compare two bottles: one full of water, one empty.
  5. Children pick two objects each and tell the class which is heavier.

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-06): Measures and estimates length, weight and capacity; converts between larger and smaller units

Activity (MEA-06-A2): Kilogram and gram, 12 min. Materials: bag of rice (1 kg), small objects, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Hold a 1 kg bag of rice (or a known weight).
  2. Ask: is your slate heavier or lighter than 1 kg?
  3. Tell children: 1 kg = 1000 grams.
  4. Show a small object that weighs about 100 grams.
  5. Ask: how many of these make 1 kg?
  6. Children list 3 things lighter than 1 kg and 3 heavier on slates.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A2): Train timetable, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a simple timetable on the board: 3 trains with departure and arrival times.
  2. Ask: how long does Train 1 take? (arrival minus departure).
  3. Which train is fastest?
  4. If you arrive at the station at 2:15 PM, which train can you catch?
  5. Children solve 2 similar problems on slates.

Check: What time does this clock show?
