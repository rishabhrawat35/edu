Day 101: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

The teacher writes 3 + 4 on the board; the class solves aloud.

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-10): Divides a number by a one-digit number and links division to multiplication

Activity (OPS-10-A1): Division with sharing, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 84 divided by 4 on the board.
  2. How many 4s in 8? Two. Write 2 above the 8. 2x4=8. Subtract: 0. Bring down 4. How many 4s in 4? One. Write 1. Answer: 21.
  3. Children solve on slates: 96 divided by 3, 72 divided by 6, 85 divided by 5.
  4. Check by multiplication: 21 x 4 = 84. Children verify one answer this way.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-17): Does the four operations on large numbers and estimates answers

Activity (OPS-17-A1): Large number operations, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 24568 + 37845 on the board in columns.
  2. Add from right to left with carrying. Walk through each column. Answer: 62413.
  3. Children solve on slates: 45230 + 18976, 62500 - 28743.
  4. Estimate first: round to nearest thousand. 45000 + 19000 = 64000. Actual: 64206. Close enough? Yes.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A2): Simple ratio problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: Mix paint in the ratio 2:3 (2 parts yellow to 3 parts blue). If you use 6 spoons of yellow, how many blue?
  2. 2 parts = 6 spoons, so 1 part = 3 spoons. 3 parts = 9 spoons blue.
  3. Children solve: ratio 3:4, if first quantity is 12, what is the second? (12÷3=4, 4x4=16.)
  4. Real-life: recipe uses 2 cups flour to 1 cup sugar. For 6 cups flour, how much sugar? 3 cups.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
