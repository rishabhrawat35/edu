Day 104: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate: how many hand-spans is the teacher's desk?

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A2): Duration challenge, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: School starts at 10:00 and ends at 3:30. How many hours?
  2. Count together on the clock.
  3. Write 3 more duration problems: lunch is 12:00 to 12:30 (how many minutes?); a movie starts at 4:00 and lasts 2 hours (when does it end?).
  4. Children solve on slates.
  5. Check answers together.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A2): Unit matching game, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 8 measurements on the board in mixed units: 3000 m, 3 km, 2000 g, 2 kg, 500 cm, 5 m, 120 min, 2 hours.
  2. Children draw lines to match equal amounts on slates.
  3. Check together.
  4. Add 4 harder pairs (e.g. 1.5 km = 1500 m).
  5. Discuss: which unit is better for each situation?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A2): Area formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle on a grid: 5 long, 3 wide.
  2. Count squares = 15. Shorter way: 5 x 3 = 15.
  3. Write: Area = length x width.
  4. For a square: Area = side x side.
  5. Children solve 4 area problems on slates.
  6. Ask: if the perimeter is 20 cm, what could the area be? (Many answers possible.)

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
