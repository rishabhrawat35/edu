Day 140: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate how many cups of water fill a bucket.

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A3): Recipe scaling, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a recipe on the board: 500 g flour, 250 ml milk, 100 g sugar (makes 1 batch).
  2. Ask: how much of each for 2 batches? For half a batch?
  3. Children calculate on slates.
  4. Write answers in both g and kg, ml and l where possible.
  5. Discuss: when does a cook think in grams and when in kilograms?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-13): Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units

Activity (MEA-13-A3): Real-life volumes, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: a teaspoon = about 5 ml, a cup = about 250 ml, a bucket = about 10 litres.
  2. Ask: how many cups in a bucket? (40). How many teaspoons in a cup? (50).
  3. Children solve 3 problems on slates mixing ml and litres.
  4. Ask: how many litres of water does your family use in a day? Estimate.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A1): Rectangle perimeter formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle: length 6 cm, width 4 cm on the board.
  2. Perimeter = 6+4+6+4 = 20 cm. Shorter way: 2 x (6+4) = 20.
  3. Write the formula: P = 2 x (l + w).
  4. Give 4 rectangles with different lengths and widths.
  5. Children use the formula on slates. Check by counting.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
