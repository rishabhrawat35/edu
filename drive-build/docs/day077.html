Day 77: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Story problem: "I had 5 mangoes and ate 2, how many left?"

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-06): Shares objects equally into groups and understands basic division

Activity (OPS-06-A2): Plate sharing game, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw 3 circles (plates) on the board.
  2. Teacher has 18 chalk marks (representing rotis). Distribute equally into the 3 plates: put one mark in each plate going round.
  3. Count: each plate has 6. Write 18 divided by 3 = 6.
  4. Erase and draw 4 plates. Distribute 20 marks. Each plate gets 5.
  5. Children do 3 problems on slate: 16 among 4, 15 among 5, 24 among 3.

Check: Share these equally - how many in each group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-13): Multiplies and divides multi-digit numbers by one-digit and two-digit numbers

Activity (OPS-13-A2): Long division practice, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 156 divided by 12 on the board.
  2. How many 12s in 15? One. 1x12=12. 15-12=3. Bring down 6. How many 12s in 36? Three. 3x12=36. 36-36=0. Answer: 13.
  3. Children solve: 208 ÷ 16, 345 ÷ 15, 252 ÷ 14.
  4. Check one answer by multiplication: 13 x 12 = 156.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-20): Solves everyday problems using the four operations, fractions and decimals

Activity (OPS-20-A2): Fraction and decimal mix, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: 1/2 + 0.3 = ?. Convert: 1/2 = 0.5. So 0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8.
  2. Write: 0.75 - 1/4 = ?. Convert: 1/4 = 0.25. So 0.75 - 0.25 = 0.50.
  3. Children solve 4 mixed problems: 1/5 + 0.4, 0.6 - 1/4, 3/4 + 0.5, 1.2 - 2/5.
  4. Rule: convert everything to the same form (all decimals or all fractions) then compute.

Check: Show me half of this. Now show one-fourth.
