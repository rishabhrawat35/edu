Day 97: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Show me a coin - what is it worth?

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A1): Clock reading drill, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a clock on the board showing 2:30.
  2. Ask: what time is it? How do you know?
  3. Change to 2:45 — ask again.
  4. Show 5 different times (mix of :00, :15, :30, :45) — children write each on slates.
  5. Ask: how many minutes from 2:30 to 3:00?

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A1): Conversion chain, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: 2 hours 30 minutes = ___ minutes. Solve: 2x60 + 30 = 150.
  2. Write: 3 km 500 m = ___ m. Solve: 3x1000 + 500 = 3500.
  3. Write: 4500 g = ___ kg ___ g. Solve: 4 kg 500 g.
  4. Children solve 5 mixed-unit conversion problems on slates.
  5. Check answers on the board.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A1): Rectangle perimeter formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle: length 6 cm, width 4 cm on the board.
  2. Perimeter = 6+4+6+4 = 20 cm. Shorter way: 2 x (6+4) = 20.
  3. Write the formula: P = 2 x (l + w).
  4. Give 4 rectangles with different lengths and widths.
  5. Children use the formula on slates. Check by counting.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
