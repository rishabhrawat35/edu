Day 32: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Raise your hand if you walked to school; keep it down if you came another way.

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-02): Sorts objects into groups by a property and reads simple pictographs

Activity (DAT-02-A2): Leaf sorting, 12 min. Materials: leaves, blackboard, chalk

  1. Collect 20 leaves from outside.
  2. Sort by size: big, medium, small.
  3. Count each group and write on the board.
  4. Draw a pictograph: one leaf drawing = 2 real leaves.
  5. Ask: how many leaves in the biggest group? How do you know from the picture?

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-04): Reads and draws bar graphs and tables and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-04-A2): Read the bar graph, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a bar graph on the board showing rainfall in 4 months (July 20 cm, Aug 25 cm, Sep 15 cm, Oct 5 cm).
  2. Ask: which month had the most rain? The least?
  3. How much more rain in August than September?
  4. What is the total rainfall?
  5. Children write answers on slates and check together.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-06): Finds and describes number patterns and extends them

Activity (DAT-06-A2): Pattern in tables, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the multiplication table of 9 on the board: 9, 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90.
  2. Ask: what do you notice about the digits? (tens go up by 1, ones go down by 1, digits always add to 9).
  3. Check: does the same pattern work for the table of 3? (digits add to 3, 6, 9, 3, 6, 9...).
  4. Children look for patterns in the table of 5 and write them on slates.
  5. Discuss: why do patterns appear in multiplication tables?

Check: What comes next in this pattern?
