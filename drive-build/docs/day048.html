Day 48: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

What day is today? What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-03): Recognises coins and notes and does simple money addition and subtraction

Activity (MEA-03-A3): Note and coin match, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a 10-rupee note and a 20-rupee note on the board.
  2. Ask: how many 5-rupee coins make 10 rupees? How many 10-rupee coins make 20?
  3. Write 3 amounts on the board: 15 Rs, 25 Rs, 50 Rs.
  4. Children write on slates which coins and notes they would use for each.
  5. Compare answers — is there more than one way?

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-08): Works with money including bills, change and simple transactions

Activity (MEA-08-A3): Budget game, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Say: you have 100 rupees to buy supplies for a party.
  2. Write 6 items with prices on the board.
  3. Children pick items that add up to as close to 100 as possible without going over.
  4. Write their choices and totals on slates.
  5. Who got closest to 100?

Check: How much money is this?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A3): Recipe scaling, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a recipe on the board: 500 g flour, 250 ml milk, 100 g sugar (makes 1 batch).
  2. Ask: how much of each for 2 batches? For half a batch?
  3. Children calculate on slates.
  4. Write answers in both g and kg, ml and l where possible.
  5. Discuss: when does a cook think in grams and when in kilograms?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?
