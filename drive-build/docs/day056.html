Day 56: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Times-table chant: class recites the 2s table together.

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-04): Solves everyday word problems using addition and subtraction of two-digit numbers

Activity (OPS-04-A2): Story chain, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk

  1. Start a story: Ravi has 56 marbles.
  2. Ask one child to continue: what happens next? (e.g. he gives 19 to his friend).
  3. All children write the subtraction on their slate and solve.
  4. Ask the next child: what happens after that? (e.g. he finds 8 more).
  5. All children add. Continue for 4-5 rounds.
  6. At the end ask: how many marbles does Ravi have now?

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-11): Solves everyday word problems using all four operations

Activity (OPS-11-A2): Two-step story chain, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Tell a story in two steps: Priya has 56 rupees. She buys 3 pencils at 8 rupees each. How much did she spend? How much is left?
  2. Step 1: 3 x 8 = 24. Step 2: 56 - 24 = 32.
  3. Give 3 more two-step problems. Children write both steps on slate.
  4. One child explains their working to the class for each problem.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-18): Adds, subtracts and compares fractions with like and unlike denominators

Activity (OPS-18-A2): Comparing unlike fractions, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: which is bigger, 2/3 or 3/5? Find common denominator: 15. Convert: 2/3=10/15, 3/5=9/15. So 2/3 > 3/5.
  2. Children compare 4 pairs on slates: 1/2 vs 2/5, 3/4 vs 2/3, 5/6 vs 4/5, 1/3 vs 2/7.
  3. Shortcut (teach ONLY after children are confident with common denominators): cross-multiply to compare. 2/3 vs 3/5: 2x5=10 vs 3x3=9. 10>9 so 2/3 wins.

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?
