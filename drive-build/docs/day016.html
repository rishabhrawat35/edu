Day 16: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher shows a row of shapes; children spot the pattern.

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-01): Extends simple patterns made of shapes or numbers

Activity (DAT-01-A3): Shape pattern strip, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a pattern on the board: circle, square, circle, square.
  2. Ask 3 children to draw the next 3 shapes.
  3. Make it harder: circle, square, triangle, circle, square, triangle.
  4. Children draw a pattern using 2 or 3 shapes on slates and extend it to 10 shapes.
  5. Ask: how do you know what comes next?

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-03): Reads and draws simple tables and pictographs and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-03-A3): Question from a table, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a table on the board: 4 villages, number of schools in each.
  2. Ask 5 questions: Which village has the most schools? How many schools in total? How many more does village A have than village C?
  3. Children answer on slates.
  4. Children make up 2 questions of their own about the table.
  5. Partners swap and answer each other's questions.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-05): Draws and reads bar graphs and tables and finds simple averages

Activity (DAT-05-A3): Temperature table, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a table: city names and temperatures for one week (made-up data for 5 cities, 5 days).
  2. Ask: find the average temperature for Delhi.
  3. Which city is hottest on average? Coolest?
  4. Draw a bar graph of average temperatures for each city.
  5. Children calculate and draw on slates.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?
