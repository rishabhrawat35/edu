Day 42: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Finger addition: teacher shows fingers on both hands, class adds.

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-03): Adds and subtracts two-digit numbers with and without carrying or borrowing

Activity (OPS-03-A3): Speed addition relay, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk

  1. Divide class into two teams.
  2. Write a two-digit addition problem on the board.
  3. First child from each team runs to the board and solves it. Other children solve the same problem on their slates while the runners are at the board.
  4. If correct that team gets a point. If wrong the next child can try.
  5. Alternate between addition with carrying and subtraction with borrowing.
  6. Play 6 rounds.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-10): Divides a number by a one-digit number and links division to multiplication

Activity (OPS-10-A3): Fact family triangles, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a triangle on the board. A fact family is a group of related multiplication and division facts using the same three numbers. Write 24 at the top, 6 on the left, 4 on the right.
  2. Write: 6x4=24, 4x6=24, 24÷6=4, 24÷4=6. These are a fact family.
  3. Draw another triangle: top 35, left 7, right ?. Children find 5. Write the 4 facts.
  4. Draw 3 more triangles with one number missing. Children complete them and write all 4 facts on slate.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-17): Does the four operations on large numbers and estimates answers

Activity (OPS-17-A3): Large multiplication and division, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 4523 x 6 on board. Multiply step by step. Answer: 27138.
  2. Write 8460 ÷ 12. Divide step by step. Answer: 705.
  3. Children solve 2 multiplication and 2 division problems on slates.
  4. Check one by doing the inverse operation.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
