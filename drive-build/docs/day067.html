Day 67: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Quick quiz: how many days in a week? How many months in a year?

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-04): Measures length, weight and capacity using simple standard units (cm, m, kg, litre)

Activity (MEA-04-A3): Weight balance, 15 min. Materials: sticks, stones, bottle caps, string, bags, 1 kg bag of rice or salt

  1. Make a simple balance with a stick across a stone pivot and two bags on each end.
  2. Put 5 stones in one bag.
  3. Ask: how many bottle caps balance 5 stones?
  4. Now use a standard weight — a 1 kg bag of rice or salt works well as the standard.
  5. Discuss: standard units mean everyone gets the same answer.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-09): Measures and converts units of length (cm, m, km), weight (g, kg) and capacity (ml, l)

Activity (MEA-09-A3): Weight and volume shop, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: 1 packet of salt = 500 g. How many packets to make 2 kg?
  2. 1 bottle of oil = 500 ml. How many bottles to make 3 litres?
  3. Children solve 4 problems on slates mixing kg/g and l/ml.
  4. Check answers together.
  5. Discuss: when do shopkeepers use kg and when g?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-13): Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units

Activity (MEA-13-A3): Real-life volumes, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: a teaspoon = about 5 ml, a cup = about 250 ml, a bucket = about 10 litres.
  2. Ask: how many cups in a bucket? (40). How many teaspoons in a cup? (50).
  3. Children solve 3 problems on slates mixing ml and litres.
  4. Ask: how many litres of water does your family use in a day? Estimate.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?
