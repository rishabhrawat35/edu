Day 29: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Estimate: how many hand-spans is the teacher's desk?

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-02): Names days of the week, sorts events by time (before, after, today, tomorrow)

Activity (MEA-02-A2): Before and after cards, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw 4 simple pictures on the board: wake up, eat breakfast, go to school, come home.
  2. Ask children to put them in order.
  3. Use words: before, after, first, then, last.
  4. Ask: what do you do before school? What do you do after school?
  5. Children draw 3 events from their day on slates and number them 1-2-3.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-07): Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes

Activity (MEA-07-A2): Duration challenge, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on the board: School starts at 10:00 and ends at 3:30. How many hours?
  2. Count together on the clock.
  3. Write 3 more duration problems: lunch is 12:00 to 12:30 (how many minutes?); a movie starts at 4:00 and lasts 2 hours (when does it end?).
  4. Children solve on slates.
  5. Check answers together.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-11): Finds the perimeter of simple shapes and the area of shapes by counting squares

Activity (MEA-11-A2): Square counting area, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a 4x3 grid of squares on the board.
  2. Ask: how many small squares? Count them — 12. This is the area.
  3. Draw an L-shape on the grid. Count the squares inside.
  4. Children draw 3 shapes on their slates on a grid and count squares for area.
  5. Ask: can two different shapes have the same area?

Check: Count these objects - how many are there?
