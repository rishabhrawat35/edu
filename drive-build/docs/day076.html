Day 76: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Doubles game: teacher says a number, class says its double.

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-06): Shares objects equally into groups and understands basic division

Activity (OPS-06-A1): Fair sharing with stones, 15 min. Materials: stones, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Give a pile of 12 stones to a group of 4 children.
  2. Ask: share these equally. Each child takes one stone at a time going around.
  3. How many does each child get? 3. Write on board: 12 shared among 4 = 3 each.
  4. Now try 15 stones among 3 children.
  5. Try 20 stones among 5 children.
  6. Discuss: what if 13 stones among 4? One is left over. That is the remainder.

Check: Share these equally - how many in each group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-13): Multiplies and divides multi-digit numbers by one-digit and two-digit numbers

Activity (OPS-13-A1): Long multiplication step by step, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 143 x 12 on the board.
  2. First multiply 143 x 2: 2x3=6, 2x4=8, 2x1=2. Write 286.
  3. Then multiply 143 x 10: write 1430 (add a zero).
  4. Add 286 + 1430 = 1716.
  5. Children solve on slates: 215 x 13, 324 x 21, 156 x 14.
  6. Two children show their full working on the board.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-20): Solves everyday problems using the four operations, fractions and decimals

Activity (OPS-20-A1): Mixed operation word problems, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A farmer has 3.5 kg of rice. He buys 2.5 kg more. Then he uses 1/4 of the total for cooking. How much rice is left?
  2. Step 1: 3.5 + 2.5 = 6 kg. Step 2: 1/4 of 6 = 6 divided by 4 = 1.5 kg used. Step 3: 6 - 1.5 = 4.5 kg left.
  3. Discuss: the answer is a clean decimal because we chose numbers carefully. In real life, answers may not always be clean.
  4. Children solve 2 similar problems on slates using whole or half numbers.

Check: Show me half of this. Now show one-fourth.
