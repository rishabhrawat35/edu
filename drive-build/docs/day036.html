Day 36: Numbers and Place Value

Whole class opening (5 min)

Count backwards from 30 together.

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (NUM-03): Compares and orders numbers up to 99 (more, less, equal, smallest to largest)

Activity (NUM-03-A1): Number line ordering, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a number line from 0 to 100 on the board (mark tens only).
  2. Write 5 numbers on the board (e.g. 23 45 12 78 56).
  3. Ask children to copy them on their slates in order from smallest to largest.
  4. One child comes to the board and places each number on the number line.
  5. Ask: which is the greatest? Which is the smallest? How do you know?

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?

Group B, Middle

Skill (NUM-06): Reads, writes and compares numbers up to 9999 using place value

Activity (NUM-06-A1): Four-digit place value tower, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw 4 columns on the board: TH H T O (thousands hundreds tens ones).
  2. Write 4826 and fill the columns together.
  3. Give children slates. Dictate 5 four-digit numbers. Children draw columns and fill them.
  4. Ask: which digit tells you the thousands?
  5. Write two four-digit numbers. Ask: which is bigger? How do you know?
  6. Children explain using place value.

Check: Read this number and write the next one.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (NUM-09): Finds factors, multiples and uses simple divisibility tests

Activity (NUM-09-A1): Factor finding with stones, 15 min. Materials: stones, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Give each child 12 stones. Ask: can you split them into equal groups of 2? Of 3? Of 4? Of 5? Of 6?
  2. Write on the board which splits work: 12 = 2 x 6 = 3 x 4. So 1 2 3 4 6 12 are factors of 12.
  3. Now give 18 stones. Children find all factors.
  4. Do one more number (24).
  5. Ask: what numbers are factors of both 12 and 18?

Check: What is this number times that number?
