Day 134: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

What day is today? What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A1): Conversion chain, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: 2 hours 30 minutes = ___ minutes. Solve: 2x60 + 30 = 150.
  2. Write: 3 km 500 m = ___ m. Solve: 3x1000 + 500 = 3500.
  3. Write: 4500 g = ___ kg ___ g. Solve: 4 kg 500 g.
  4. Children solve 5 mixed-unit conversion problems on slates.
  5. Check answers on the board.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-13): Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units

Activity (MEA-13-A1): Volume with cubes, 15 min. Materials: stones or bottle caps, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Stack stones or bottle caps in rows to build a 2x3x2 block.
  2. Count total: 12 units. This is the volume.
  3. Build a different shape with the same 12 units.
  4. Children build their own block on the desk and count units.
  5. Write: volume = length x width x height. Check with the count.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A2): Area formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle on a grid: 5 long, 3 wide.
  2. Count squares = 15. Shorter way: 5 x 3 = 15.
  3. Write: Area = length x width.
  4. For a square: Area = side x side.
  5. Children solve 4 area problems on slates.
  6. Ask: if the perimeter is 20 cm, what could the area be? (Many answers possible.)

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
