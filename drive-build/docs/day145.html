Day 145: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Finger addition: teacher shows fingers on both hands, class adds.

Rotation

Teacher guides C (15 min); then B (15 min); then A (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-19): Reads, writes, compares and adds decimals; converts between simple fractions and decimals

Activity (OPS-19-A1): Fraction to decimal conversion, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write on board: 1/2 = 0.5 (divide 1 by 2). 1/4 = 0.25 (divide 1 by 4). 1/5 = 0.2 (divide 1 by 5).
  2. Show the division: 1.00 ÷ 4 = 0.25.
  3. Children convert on slates: 3/4, 2/5, 7/10, 1/8.
  4. Reverse: 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4. 0.6 = 6/10 = 3/5.
  5. Children convert 3 decimals to fractions.

Check: Read this number and write the next one.

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-20): Solves everyday problems using the four operations, fractions and decimals

Activity (OPS-20-A1): Mixed operation word problems, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A farmer has 3.5 kg of rice. He buys 2.5 kg more. Then he uses 1/4 of the total for cooking. How much rice is left?
  2. Step 1: 3.5 + 2.5 = 6 kg. Step 2: 1/4 of 6 = 6 divided by 4 = 1.5 kg used. Step 3: 6 - 1.5 = 4.5 kg left.
  3. Discuss: the answer is a clean decimal because we chose numbers carefully. In real life, answers may not always be clean.
  4. Children solve 2 similar problems on slates using whole or half numbers.

Check: Show me half of this. Now show one-fourth.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A3): Speed and distance problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: A bus travels 45 km in 1 hour. How far in 3 hours? 45 x 3 = 135 km. How far in half an hour? 45 ÷ 2 = 22.5 km.
  2. Reverse: A car travels 180 km in 4 hours. Speed per hour? 180 ÷ 4 = 45 km/hour.
  3. Children solve 3 problems on slates: distance-speed-time using unitary method.
  4. Discuss: this is the same unitary method — find the value for 1 hour then multiply or divide.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
