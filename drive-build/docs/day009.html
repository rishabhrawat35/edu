Day 9: Shapes and Space

Whole class opening (5 min)

Point to something round, something square, something triangular.

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (GEO-01): Names and describes basic 2-D and 3-D shapes from their features (sides, corners, faces)

Activity (GEO-01-A3): Body shapes, 10 min. Materials: string, blackboard, chalk

  1. Have 3 children stand and hold a string to form a triangle.
  2. Have 4 children form a square.
  3. Ask the class: how many children make a triangle? A square?
  4. Ask one child to curl the string into a circle — how many corners?
  5. Draw each shape on the board and label sides and corners.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (GEO-03): Describes 2-D and 3-D shapes in detail and finds lines of symmetry in simple figures

Activity (GEO-03-A3): Mirror shapes, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw half a butterfly on the board along a vertical line.
  2. Ask children to complete the other half so it is symmetrical.
  3. Children draw half a shape on their slates and exchange with a partner.
  4. Partner completes the other half.
  5. Check together: fold an imaginary line — do both halves match?

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (GEO-05): Finds symmetry in shapes and extends patterns involving shapes and numbers

Activity (GEO-05-A3): Tile the floor, 15 min. Materials: paper, leaves, slates, chalk

  1. Cut paper into equal squares (or use leaves of similar size).
  2. Show how squares tile a flat surface with no gaps.
  3. Try with triangles — do they tile?
  4. Try with circles — do they tile?
  5. On slates, children draw a pattern that tiles using one shape.
  6. Discuss: why do some shapes tile and others don't?

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
