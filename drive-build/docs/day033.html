Day 33: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Raise your hand if you walked to school; keep it down if you came another way.

Rotation

Teacher starts with group C (15 min); then guides A (15 min); then guides B (15 min). Other groups do independent practice.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-02): Sorts objects into groups by a property and reads simple pictographs

Activity (DAT-02-A3): Favourite fruit pictograph, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk

  1. Ask each child: what is your favourite fruit — mango, banana, or guava?
  2. Write names under each fruit on the board.
  3. Count each column.
  4. Draw a pictograph: one smiley face = 1 child.
  5. Ask: which fruit is most popular? How many children chose banana? How many more chose mango than guava?

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-04): Reads and draws bar graphs and tables and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-04-A3): Collect and graph, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Give each child a task: count the number of windows, doors, and benches in the school.
  2. Children report numbers — write on the board.
  3. Together draw a bar graph of the data.
  4. Choose a scale that fits (each square = 1 or = 2).
  5. Ask: why did we choose that scale? What if each square was 5?

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-06): Finds and describes number patterns and extends them

Activity (DAT-06-A3): Create your own pattern, 12 min. Materials: slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Each child creates a number pattern with a hidden rule and writes 5 terms on their slate.
  2. Pass the slate to a partner.
  3. The partner writes the next 3 terms and the rule.
  4. Check together.
  5. Share the hardest patterns with the class on the board.
  6. Vote on the trickiest pattern.

Check: What comes next in this pattern?
