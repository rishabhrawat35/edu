Day 83: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

What day is today? What day was yesterday? What day is tomorrow?

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-05): Reads time to the hour and half hour and knows days, weeks and months

Activity (MEA-05-A2): Daily schedule clock, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. List 5 daily activities on the board (wake up, school starts, lunch, school ends, sleep).
  2. Write the approximate time next to each.
  3. Draw a clock for each time.
  4. Ask: which activity happens first? Which happens at half past?
  5. Children write their own 3-activity schedule with clock drawings on slates.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A2): Train timetable, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a simple timetable on the board: 3 trains with departure and arrival times.
  2. Ask: how long does Train 1 take? (arrival minus departure).
  3. Which train is fastest?
  4. If you arrive at the station at 2:15 PM, which train can you catch?
  5. Children solve 2 similar problems on slates.

Check: What time does this clock show?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-14): Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas

Activity (MEA-14-A2): Area formula, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a rectangle on a grid: 5 long, 3 wide.
  2. Count squares = 15. Shorter way: 5 x 3 = 15.
  3. Write: Area = length x width.
  4. For a square: Area = side x side.
  5. Children solve 4 area problems on slates.
  6. Ask: if the perimeter is 20 cm, what could the area be? (Many answers possible.)

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
