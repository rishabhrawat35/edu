Day 14: Data Handling and Patterns

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher shows a row of shapes; children spot the pattern.

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (DAT-01): Extends simple patterns made of shapes or numbers

Activity (DAT-01-A1): Clap pattern, 10 min. Materials: children's bodies

  1. Clap a pattern: clap-clap-snap-clap-clap-snap.
  2. Ask children to repeat.
  3. Ask: what comes next?
  4. Change the pattern: clap-snap-snap-clap-snap-snap.
  5. Children create their own clap pattern and a partner continues it.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (DAT-03): Reads and draws simple tables and pictographs and answers questions from them

Activity (DAT-03-A1): Class birthday table, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a table on the board: Month and Number of birthdays.
  2. Ask each child their birthday month.
  3. Make tally marks for each month.
  4. Convert tallies to numbers.
  5. Ask: which month has the most birthdays? How many children have birthdays in January?
  6. Children copy the table onto slates.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (DAT-05): Draws and reads bar graphs and tables and finds simple averages

Activity (DAT-05-A1): Find the average, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 5 numbers on the board: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.
  2. The average (also called the mean) tells us what each person would get if we shared everything equally. Add them: 40. Divide by 5 (how many numbers there are): 8. This is the average.
  3. Explain: the average tells us what is typical.
  4. Write heights of 5 children (in cm) on the board. Find the average height.
  5. Children solve 3 average problems on slates.

Check: Look at this chart - how many in this group?
