Day 46: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Show me a coin - what is it worth?

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-03): Recognises coins and notes and does simple money addition and subtraction

Activity (MEA-03-A1): Coin sorting, 12 min. Materials: coins or drawn coin pictures, blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Show real or drawn coins: 1, 2, 5, 10 rupees.
  2. Children identify each coin.
  3. Ask: how many 1-rupee coins make 5 rupees? How many 2-rupee coins make 10?
  4. Draw items with prices on the board (pencil 5 Rs, eraser 3 Rs).
  5. Children pick coins from a set to pay the exact amount for each item.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-08): Works with money including bills, change and simple transactions

Activity (MEA-08-A1): Market trip math, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates, coins

  1. Write a menu on the board: dal 40 Rs, rice 30 Rs, oil 55 Rs, soap 20 Rs.
  2. A family buys dal and rice — what is the total?
  3. They pay with a 100-rupee note — how much change?
  4. Children solve 3 more buying problems on slates.
  5. One child plays shopkeeper and gives correct change using coins.

Check: How much money is this?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A1): Conversion chain, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: 2 hours 30 minutes = ___ minutes. Solve: 2x60 + 30 = 150.
  2. Write: 3 km 500 m = ___ m. Solve: 3x1000 + 500 = 3500.
  3. Write: 4500 g = ___ kg ___ g. Solve: 4 kg 500 g.
  4. Children solve 5 mixed-unit conversion problems on slates.
  5. Check answers on the board.

Check: Measure this - how long is it?
