Day 7: Shapes and Space

Whole class opening (5 min)

Look around the room - name every shape you can see.

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (GEO-01): Names and describes basic 2-D and 3-D shapes from their features (sides, corners, faces)

Activity (GEO-01-A1): Shape hunt, 10 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk

  1. Draw a circle, triangle, square and rectangle on the board.
  2. Say each name aloud and have children repeat.
  3. Send children in pairs to find one object in the room that matches each shape.
  4. Each pair shows their objects and names the shape.
  5. Ask: how many sides does a triangle have? A square?

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group B, Middle

Skill (GEO-03): Describes 2-D and 3-D shapes in detail and finds lines of symmetry in simple figures

Activity (GEO-03-A1): Symmetry fold, 15 min. Materials: paper, slates, chalk, blackboard

  1. Give each child a piece of paper.
  2. Fold it in half and tear out a shape along the fold.
  3. Open it — both halves match.
  4. Draw a line down the middle on the board and show this is the line of symmetry.
  5. Children draw 3 shapes on their slates and try to draw a line of symmetry on each.
  6. Discuss: does a circle have a line of symmetry? How many?

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (GEO-05): Finds symmetry in shapes and extends patterns involving shapes and numbers

Activity (GEO-05-A1): Pattern builder, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a repeating shape pattern on the board: circle, square, triangle, circle, square, ?
  2. Ask children to predict the next three shapes.
  3. Now make a number-shape pattern: 1 circle, 2 squares, 3 triangles, ?
  4. Children create their own pattern on slates with at least 3 repeats.
  5. Partners exchange slates and extend each other's pattern.

Check: Name this shape and count its sides.
