Day 125: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Story problem: "I had 5 mangoes and ate 2, how many left?"

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-13): Multiplies and divides multi-digit numbers by one-digit and two-digit numbers

Activity (OPS-13-A3): Multiply and check, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 3 multiplication problems on the board: 27x15, 34x23, 46x18.
  2. Children solve on slates.
  3. For each answer, teacher writes a division to check: e.g. 405 ÷ 15 should give 27.
  4. Children do the division and verify. If the numbers do not match, find the mistake.
  5. Discuss: multiplication and division are inverse operations.

Check: What is this number times that number?

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-20): Solves everyday problems using the four operations, fractions and decimals

Activity (OPS-20-A3): Real-life calculation sheet, 15 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a scenario on board: School trip costs Rs 125 per child. 24 children go. How much total? Then 1/3 of children bring their own lunch. The rest eat at Rs 45 each. What is the total food cost?
  2. Children plan the steps before calculating. Write the steps in words first.
  3. Then compute.
  4. Share and discuss whether answers are reasonable.

Check: Show me half of this. Now show one-fourth.

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A2): Simple ratio problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: Mix paint in the ratio 2:3 (2 parts yellow to 3 parts blue). If you use 6 spoons of yellow, how many blue?
  2. 2 parts = 6 spoons, so 1 part = 3 spoons. 3 parts = 9 spoons blue.
  3. Children solve: ratio 3:4, if first quantity is 12, what is the second? (12÷3=4, 4x4=16.)
  4. Real-life: recipe uses 2 cups flour to 1 cup sugar. For 6 cups flour, how much sugar? 3 cups.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
