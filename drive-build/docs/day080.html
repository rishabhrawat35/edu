Day 80: Operations

Whole class opening (5 min)

Teacher gives a multiplication; children show the answer with fingers.

Rotation

Teacher guides group A (15 min) while B and C practise independently; then guides B (15 min); then guides C (15 min).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (OPS-07): Identifies half and quarter of a whole object or collection

Activity (OPS-07-A2): Sharing fruit equally, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, stones, slates

  1. Draw a circle on the board (a roti). Draw a line through the middle. Ask: if 2 children share this roti equally, how much does each get? One half. Write 1/2.
  2. Draw another roti. Cut it into 4 equal parts. Each child gets one quarter. Write 1/4.
  3. Give children 12 stones. Find half of 12: divide into 2 equal groups. Half of 12 = 6.
  4. Find quarter of 12: divide into 4 groups. Quarter of 12 = 3.

Check: Show me: Identifies half and quarter of a whole object or c

Group B, Middle

Skill (OPS-14): Solves multi-step everyday problems using the four operations

Activity (OPS-14-A2): Reverse problem solving, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write the answer on the board: 360 rupees.
  2. Ask: make up a two-step word problem whose answer is 360. Children write their problem on slate.
  3. Share 3 different problems aloud. The class checks if each one really gives 360.
  4. Teacher gives another answer: 150. Children create another problem.
  5. Discuss which problems had the most interesting stories.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (OPS-21): Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations

Activity (OPS-21-A2): Simple ratio problems, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write: Mix paint in the ratio 2:3 (2 parts yellow to 3 parts blue). If you use 6 spoons of yellow, how many blue?
  2. 2 parts = 6 spoons, so 1 part = 3 spoons. 3 parts = 9 spoons blue.
  3. Children solve: ratio 3:4, if first quantity is 12, what is the second? (12÷3=4, 4x4=16.)
  4. Real-life: recipe uses 2 cups flour to 1 cup sugar. For 6 cups flour, how much sugar? 3 cups.

Check: If 3 pencils cost 15 rupees, what do 5 cost?
