Day 47: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Quick quiz: how many days in a week? How many months in a year?

Rotation

Teacher starts with the group that struggled yesterday (15 min); then rotates to the other two (15 min each).

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-03): Recognises coins and notes and does simple money addition and subtraction

Activity (MEA-03-A2): Shop game, 15 min. Materials: coins or paper money, items with price tags, blackboard, chalk

  1. Set up a pretend shop: 4 items with price tags (chalk 2 Rs, paper 5 Rs, string 3 Rs, stone 1 Rs).
  2. One child is the shopkeeper.
  3. A buyer chooses 2 items and adds the prices.
  4. The buyer pays with coins and the shopkeeper gives change if needed.
  5. Rotate roles. Write each sum on the board.

Check: Solve this addition and this subtraction.

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-08): Works with money including bills, change and simple transactions

Activity (MEA-08-A2): Bill checker, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write a pretend bill on the board: 3 items with prices.
  2. Add them up together.
  3. Write the total. Now check: is the total correct?
  4. Give children 3 bills on slates (some with wrong totals).
  5. They check each and mark correct or incorrect.

Check: How much money is this?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-12): Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time across all standard units

Activity (MEA-12-A2): Unit matching game, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Write 8 measurements on the board in mixed units: 3000 m, 3 km, 2000 g, 2 kg, 500 cm, 5 m, 120 min, 2 hours.
  2. Children draw lines to match equal amounts on slates.
  3. Check together.
  4. Add 4 harder pairs (e.g. 1.5 km = 1500 m).
  5. Discuss: which unit is better for each situation?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?
