Day 11: Measurement

Whole class opening (5 min)

Show me a coin - what is it worth?

Rotation

Teacher guides group B first (15 min); then C (15 min); then A (15 min). Independent practice for others.

Group A, Beginning

Skill (MEA-01): Compares objects by length, weight or capacity using everyday words and non-standard units

Activity (MEA-01-A1): Long and short lineup, 10 min. Materials: sticks

  1. Collect 5 sticks of different lengths.
  2. Ask children to arrange them from shortest to longest.
  3. Ask: which is the longest? Which is the shortest?
  4. Use words: longer than, shorter than, same as.
  5. Children find two objects in the room and tell a partner which is longer.

Check: Which number is bigger? How do you know?

Group B, Middle

Skill (MEA-06): Measures and estimates length, weight and capacity; converts between larger and smaller units

Activity (MEA-06-A1): Estimate then measure, 12 min. Materials: sticks, ruler or measuring tape, slates, chalk

  1. Hold up a stick. Ask: how many centimetres long do you think it is?
  2. Children write their estimate on slates.
  3. Measure with a ruler — write the actual length.
  4. Who was closest?
  5. Repeat with 3 more objects.
  6. Discuss: 100 cm = 1 metre. How many sticks would make 1 metre?

Check: Measure this - how long is it?

Group C, Advanced

Skill (MEA-10): Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations

Activity (MEA-10-A1): 24-hour clock, 12 min. Materials: blackboard, chalk, slates

  1. Draw a number line from 0 to 24 on the board.
  2. Mark 12 — this is noon.
  3. Write: 1 PM = 13:00, 2 PM = 14:00. Ask children to continue.
  4. Give 5 times in 12-hour format — children write in 24-hour on slates.
  5. Give 5 times in 24-hour — children write in 12-hour.

Check: What time does this clock show?
