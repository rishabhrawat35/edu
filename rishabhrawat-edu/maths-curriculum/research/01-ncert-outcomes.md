## NCERT outcomes by class

### Class I (11)
- M1-01 Counts objects up to 20 and says how many there are. [paraphrased]
- M1-02 Reads and writes numerals up to 99. [paraphrased]
- M1-03 Compares two numbers and says which is more, less or equal. [paraphrased]
- M1-04 Orders numbers up to 20 from smallest to largest and back. [paraphrased]
- M1-05 Shows tens and ones for two-digit numbers using objects or drawings. [paraphrased]
- M1-06 Adds and subtracts numbers up to 20 using objects or pictures. [paraphrased]
- M1-07 Solves simple everyday addition and subtraction problems with small numbers. [paraphrased]
- M1-08 Names and describes basic 2-D and 3-D shapes from their features. [paraphrased]
- M1-09 Extends simple patterns made of shapes or numbers. [paraphrased]
- M1-10 Compares objects by length, weight or capacity using everyday words and non-standard units. [paraphrased]
- M1-11 Names the days of the week and sorts events by time (before, after, today, tomorrow). [paraphrased]

### Class II (12)
- M2-01 Reads and writes numbers up to 999 using place value. [paraphrased]
- M2-02 Counts forward and backward, and in jumps of 2, 5 and 10. [paraphrased]
- M2-03 Compares and orders numbers up to 999. [paraphrased]
- M2-04 Adds and subtracts two-digit numbers, with and without carrying or borrowing. [paraphrased]
- M2-05 Solves everyday problems using addition and subtraction. [paraphrased]
- M2-06 Understands multiplication as repeated addition and uses tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10. [paraphrased]
- M2-07 Shares objects equally into groups to start understanding division. [paraphrased]
- M2-08 Identifies half and quarter of a whole object or collection. [paraphrased]
- M2-09 Measures length, weight and capacity using simple standard units (cm, m, kg, litre). [paraphrased]
- M2-10 Reads time to the hour and half hour, and knows days, weeks and months. [paraphrased]
- M2-11 Recognises coins and notes and does simple money addition and subtraction. [paraphrased]
- M2-12 Reads simple pictographs and sorts objects into groups by a property. [paraphrased]

### Class III (12)
- M3-01 Reads, writes and compares numbers up to 9999 using place value. [paraphrased]
- M3-02 Adds and subtracts three-digit numbers, including with carrying and borrowing. [paraphrased]
- M3-03 Multiplies a two-digit number by a one-digit number. [paraphrased]
- M3-04 Divides a number by a one-digit number and links division to multiplication. [paraphrased]
- M3-05 Knows multiplication tables up to 10. [paraphrased]
- M3-06 Solves everyday problems using the four operations. [paraphrased]
- M3-07 Understands fractions such as half, one-third and one-fourth, and compares simple fractions. [paraphrased]
- M3-08 Measures and estimates length, weight and capacity, and converts between larger and smaller units. [paraphrased]
- M3-09 Reads time in hours and minutes from a clock and finds how long something takes. [paraphrased]
- M3-10 Works with money, including bills and change. [paraphrased]
- M3-11 Describes 2-D and 3-D shapes and finds lines of symmetry in simple figures. [paraphrased]
- M3-12 Reads and draws simple tables and pictographs and answers questions from them. [paraphrased]

### Class IV (12)
- M4-01 Reads, writes and compares numbers up to five or six digits using place value. [paraphrased]
- M4-02 Adds and subtracts large numbers and estimates answers. [paraphrased]
- M4-03 Multiplies and divides multi-digit numbers by one-digit and two-digit numbers. [paraphrased]
- M4-04 Solves multi-step everyday problems using the four operations. [paraphrased]
- M4-05 Understands equivalent fractions and adds and subtracts fractions with the same denominator. [paraphrased]
- M4-06 Uses decimal notation for money and for lengths such as metres and centimetres. [paraphrased]
- M4-07 Finds the perimeter of simple shapes and the area of shapes by counting squares. [paraphrased]
- M4-08 Reads time on a 12-hour and 24-hour clock and works out durations. [paraphrased]
- M4-09 Measures and converts units of length, weight and capacity. [paraphrased]
- M4-10 Identifies angles, lines and the faces and edges of 3-D shapes. [paraphrased]
- M4-11 Finds symmetry and extends patterns involving shapes and numbers. [paraphrased]
- M4-12 Reads and draws bar graphs and tables and answers questions from them. [paraphrased]

### Class V (13)
- M5-01 Reads, writes and compares large numbers (lakhs and crores) using place value. [paraphrased]
- M5-02 Does the four operations on large numbers and estimates answers. [paraphrased]
- M5-03 Finds factors and multiples and uses divisibility tests. [paraphrased]
- M5-04 Adds, subtracts and compares fractions with like and unlike denominators. [paraphrased]
- M5-05 Reads, writes, compares and adds decimals, and converts between simple fractions and decimals. [paraphrased]
- M5-06 Solves everyday problems using the four operations, fractions and decimals. [paraphrased]
- M5-07 Finds the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares. [paraphrased]
- M5-08 Understands volume and capacity and measures them with standard units. [paraphrased]
- M5-09 Converts units of length, weight, capacity and time. [paraphrased]
- M5-10 Uses simple ratio or unitary-method thinking in everyday situations. [paraphrased]
- M5-11 Identifies and measures angles and describes properties of 2-D and 3-D shapes. [paraphrased]
- M5-12 Draws and reads bar graphs and tables, and finds simple averages. [paraphrased]
- M5-13 Finds and describes number patterns and uses simple symmetry and tiling. [paraphrased]
