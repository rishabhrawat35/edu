# Resources

Reference materials the research agents should consult, organized by subject and country. This is a starting point — the user should add links specific to their context.

## How to use this file

When filling in the `sources/` folder for a new curriculum build, gather materials from the categories below that match your subject, country, and classroom model. Not every category is required — use what is available.

---

## 1. National curriculum frameworks and learning outcomes

These define what students should know at each grade.

### India
- **NCERT Syllabus & Textbooks** — https://ncert.nic.in/ — Free textbooks for all subjects, grades 1-12. Download the grade-specific PDFs.
- **NIPUN Bharat (FLN Mission)** — https://nipunbharat.education.gov.in/ — Foundational Literacy and Numeracy goals for grades 1-3.
- **NEP 2020 document** — https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/NEP_Final_English.pdf
- **State-specific curriculum** — Each state may have its own textbook board (e.g., SCERT Maharashtra, SCERT Karnataka). Check the relevant state education department website.

### United Kingdom
- **National Curriculum** — https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-curriculum — Programmes of study for each key stage.
- **Education Endowment Foundation (EEF)** — https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/ — Evidence-based teaching guidance.

### United States
- **Common Core State Standards** — https://www.thecorestandards.org/ — Maths and English Language Arts standards by grade.
- **Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)** — https://www.nextgenscience.org/

### East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda)
- **Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD)** — https://kicd.ac.ke/ — Competency-Based Curriculum materials.
- **Tanzania Institute of Education** — https://www.tie.go.tz/

### General / Multi-country
- **UNESCO IBE Curriculum Resources** — https://www.ibe.unesco.org/ — Comparative curriculum data across countries.

## 2. Classroom models and pedagogy

These define how to teach in multi-grade, multi-level, or other specific classroom settings.

### Multi-Grade Multi-Level (MGML)
- **Das, A.K. — "MGML: A Pedagogic Approach"** — The foundational article on MGML methodology. Source the full text from education archives or Rishi Valley publications.
- **Rishi Valley / RiVER** — https://www.rishivalley.org/ — The Rishi Valley Institute of Educational Resources developed the MGML ladder approach with activity cards and milestones.

### Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL)
- **Pratham / TaRL** — https://www.teachingattherightlevel.org/ — Assessment tools, grouping methodology, and teaching materials.
- **J-PAL evidence on TaRL** — https://www.povertyactionlab.org/case-study/teaching-right-level-reach-all-students — Research backing.

### Activity-Based Learning (ABL)
- **Tamil Nadu ABL model** — Search for "Activity Based Learning Tamil Nadu" for state government documentation on the ABL card system used in primary schools.

### General multi-grade teaching
- **Little, A.W. (2006) — "Education for All and Multigrade Teaching"** — Springer. Academic reference on multi-grade pedagogy.
- **UNESCO multi-grade teaching toolkit** — https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ — Search for "multigrade teaching."

### Mathematics

- **NCERT Looking Around (grades 3-5)** — https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php

- **Early Grade Reading Assessment (EGRA) / Early Grade Maths Assessment (EGMA)** — https://www.edu-links.org/resources/early-grade-reading-assessment-egra-toolkit — USAID-developed diagnostic tools used across developing countries.

## Notes for the user

1. **Always verify links before running the pipeline.** URLs change. If a link is broken, search for the resource by name.
2. **Prioritize official sources** (government curriculum documents, textbook PDFs) over third-party interpretations.
3. **For languages other than English/Hindi**, check if the curriculum body publishes materials in the target language. Most state SCERTs in India publish in the state language.
4. **Textbook PDFs are the most important input.** Without them, the textbook researcher agent cannot inventory chapters, and the curriculum designer has to work from outcomes alone. At minimum, provide a table of contents for each grade's textbook.
5. **If no methodology source exists** for your classroom model, the pedagogy researcher will extract rules from whatever you provide. Even a 2-page description of how your school operates is useful.
